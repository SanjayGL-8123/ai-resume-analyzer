Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Welcome to AI Resume Analyzer. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we handle your personal data when using our resume analysis platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Resume Content: Extracted text from uploaded documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) including contact details, skills, and work history.
  • Job Descriptions: Role title and description details supplied for keyword matching.
  • Analysis Results: Match scores, identified skills, and missing skill metrics.

2. How Your Data Is Processed

Data processing is conducted strictly for score generation (via local TF-IDF cosine similarity) and skill extraction against our skill dictionary. If configured, records are stored in your local MySQL instance.

3. Privacy & Third-Party Protection

  • No personal data or resume files are sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • Analysis is performed locally on server runtime without third-party LLM cloud API data transmission.

4. Data Management & Retention

Users maintain full control to wipe stored upload files or clear database records from MySQL at any time.
